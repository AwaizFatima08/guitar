🎸 Always wanted to learn guitar? Start today, even if you know nothing about music.

Geetar is a free guitar course for complete beginners, made by HomiLabs. It was built for one young learner who just got her first guitar, and now it's here for everyone. 💜 Made with Love for Pari.

🎯 A tuner in plain words: "too low, tighten" or "too high, loosen"
📚 27 short lessons in three levels: Basic, Medium and Expert
🎵 Songs in tab with Play along: the app listens and moves on when you play the right note
🖐️ 29 chords you can hear, plus a metronome, a strumming trainer and practice games
🔥 Practice timer, daily goal and streak
🙅 No account and no sign-in: just enter your name

📲 Scan the QR code on the poster, or install from Google Play:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.homilabs.geetar

Prefer the browser? Try it at https://tools.homilabs.org/guitar_app/

Know someone who just got a guitar? Tag them or share this post. Feedback is very welcome in the comments.

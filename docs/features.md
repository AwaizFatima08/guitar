# Geetar features

## In version 1.0
| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tuner | Microphone pitch detection, auto or chosen string, "tighten / loosen" advice, green zone ±5 cents, reference tone per string, a tick per tuned string |
| Lessons | 27 lessons: Basic 10, Medium 9, Expert 8. Goal, steps, diagrams, sounds, quizzes, "can you do it?" checklist, mark as done |
| Basic | Parts of the guitar, holding and finger numbers, string names, tuning, first notes, reading tab and chord boxes, first song, Em, Am, strumming in time |
| Medium | G C D, E A Dm, smooth changes, strumming patterns, four-chord song, melodies, reading rhythm, power chords and palm muting, 12-bar blues |
| Expert | Barre chords, moveable shapes, minor pentatonic and improvising, hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides, fingerpicking, theory, capo and keys, colour chords |
| Chords | 29 chord boxes, tap to strum, "one by one" to check each string, filter by level |
| Songs | 10 tabs: Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Twinkle, Jingle Bells, Happy Birthday, scales and exercises. Speed control, click, repeat |
| Play along | The app listens through the microphone and moves to the next note when the right one is played |
| Practice | Metronome with tap tempo, strumming trainer, one-minute changes with best scores, fretboard explorer, find-the-note game, capo helper |
| Motivation | Practice timer, daily goal ring, day streak, stats, celebration sounds |
| Words | Glossary of 22 guitar words in plain language |
| App | Offline, installable, light and dark, no ads, no tracking, HomiLabs logo and credits, dedication to Pari |

## Suggested next steps (not built)

2. **Rhythm game**: strum on the beat, and the app scores timing from the microphone.
3. **Chord recognition** in Play along, so chord songs can be checked too (harder than single notes).
4. **Left-handed mode** that mirrors chord boxes and the fretboard.
5. **Song requests**: add songs Pari likes as chord charts (chords only, no lyrics, to avoid copyright issues).
6. **Weekly certificate** or badges at the end of each level.
7. **Urdu interface** for family members who prefer it.
8. **Android app** wrapper for Google Play, if wanted later.

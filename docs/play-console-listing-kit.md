## Store listing
- **App name (30):** Geetar: Learn Guitar
- **Short description (80):** Learn guitar from zero: tuner, easy lessons, chords, songs and practice games.
- **Full description:**

  Geetar teaches guitar to complete beginners. You don't need to know anything about music.

  🎯 TUNER IN PLAIN WORDS
  Pluck a string and Geetar tells you "too low, tighten" or "too high, loosen". Hear each string's correct note, and see a tick when it's in tune.

  📚 27 STEP-BY-STEP LESSONS IN THREE LEVELS
  • Basic: parts of the guitar, holding it, string names, tuning, first notes, reading tab and chord boxes, your first song and first chords.
  • Medium: G, C, D and more chords, smooth chord changes, strumming patterns, rhythm, power chords and the 12-bar blues.
  • Expert: barre chords, moveable shapes, the pentatonic scale, hammer-ons and slides, fingerpicking, music theory and the capo.

  🎵 SONGS WITH PLAY ALONG
  Traditional tunes in tab, such as Mary Had a Little Lamb, Ode to Joy, Twinkle Twinkle, Jingle Bells and Happy Birthday. Press Play to hear them, or Play along: Geetar listens and moves on when you play the right note.

  🖐️ CHORD LIBRARY AND PRACTICE TOOLS
  29 chord diagrams you can hear, a metronome, a strumming trainer, one-minute chord changes, a fretboard explorer, a find-the-note game and a capo helper.

  🔥 STAY MOTIVATED
  Practice timer, daily goal and streak.

  No account and no sign-in: just enter your name. Your progress stays on your phone. The microphone is used only for the tuner and Play along, and sound is never recorded.

  Made with Love for Pari. Created By: HomiLabs · Managed By: Awaiz Fatima.

## App content
- **Ads:** Yes (AdMob banner on menu screens, an interstitial at most once per two finished lessons, family-rated G).
- **App access:** All functionality is available without special access.
- **Content rating questionnaire:** Reference/education. No violence, no user interaction or sharing, no purchases. Expected rating Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience and content:** recommended **13+** (like Lingo15). Ads are G-rated but not child-directed. If you choose an audience that includes under-13s, set `kChildDirected = true` in `mobile/lib/ads.dart`, rebuild, and Play's Families policy applies.
- **Data safety:**
  - Data collected by the app itself: none (name and progress stay on the device; microphone audio is processed on the device and not stored).
  - Data collected by the AdMob SDK: device or other IDs (advertising ID), approximate location, app interactions, crash logs and diagnostics. Purpose: advertising or marketing, analytics, fraud prevention. Shared with Google. Encrypted in transit. Users can't request deletion through you (it is the ad provider's data).
  - Microphone: declare "Audio: voice or sound recordings" only if Play asks; it is processed ephemerally on the device and never collected. Choose "not collected".
- **Advertising ID:** Yes (AdMob).
- **Government / financial / health:** No.
